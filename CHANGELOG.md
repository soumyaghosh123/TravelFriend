- Build step 11 (part 1) — accounts and saved trips:
  - Sign-in with a one-time email link (15 minutes, single use; the link opens a "Continue" page so mail scanners can't use it up; at most 3 links per address per 15 minutes and 5 requests per IP per 10 minutes; the reply never reveals whether an address has an account) or with Google (OpenID Connect, authorization code + PKCE, ID token verified with Google's keys). Same verified email = same account. No passwords.
  - Sessions: random 256-bit cookie (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure on https, 30 days); only SHA-256 hashes of session and link secrets are stored. Changes are refused from other origins; "next" redirects are limited to paths on our site (API and web).
  - Saved trips (`/api/me/trips`): save the plan on screen (saving again updates it), list, delete, open (an expired search is run again from the saved inputs; a past month returns 410). "Booked" ticks sync to the account and follow you across devices; ticks made before saving are carried over.

  - Email via any SMTP service (`SMTP_URL`, `MAIL_FROM`); without it the link is logged (development). Google via `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` / `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`; without them the button is hidden.
  - Web: `/account` (sign in, My trips), `/signin/verify`, a "Sign in / My trips" header link, and "Save trip" in the share bar. en / hi / bn.
