Why some dates cost more

  • Peak Christmas–New Year (20–31 Dec): hotels cost 2–3× more and trains are waitlisted
  • Holiday Goa Liberation Day, 19 Dec: a state holiday that makes a long weekend
  • Event Feast of St Francis Xavier, 3 Dec: Old Goa is crowded and nearby rooms cost more
  • Weekends Fri–Sat nights cost about 15% more

✅ Do

  • Cover your shoulders and knees in churches and temples
  • Wear a helmet and carry your licence when you ride a rented scooter
  • Swim only between the lifeguard flags
  • Carry cash for markets and ferries. North Goa has plenty of ATMs; Netravali has few.
  • Pack sunscreen, a hat and light cotton clothes (Dec: 32° / 21°)

⛔ Don't

  • Drink alcohol or cook on beaches (you can be fined)
  • Leave plastic on the beach; single-use plastic is restricted
  • Accept drug offers; penalties under the NDPS Act are severe
  • Photograph people or naval areas without permission
  • Rent a scooter without checking it for existing damage first

🚨 Emergency

All emergencies 112 · Ambulance 108 · Nearest hospital: North Goa District Hospital, Mapusa (7 km) · Calangute police station (1.2 km)

📶 Network & permits

Good 4G/5G in North and South Goa · weak signal at Netravali and Cabo de Rama · no permits needed · Last checked 2 Nov 2026
